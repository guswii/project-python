## 🔑 Vocabulary

### Liquidity
- **BSL** = Buy-Side Liquidity (di atas swing high, stop loss卖家)
- **SSL** = Sell-Side Liquidity (di bawah swing low, stop loss买家)
- **ERL** = External Range Liquidity (swing high/low utama)
- **IRL** = Internal Range Liquidity (FVG/OB di dalam range)
- **DOL** = Draw on Liquidity (target terdekat paling obvious)

### Structure
- **BOS** = Break of Structure (continuation — break in direction of trend)
- **CHoCH** = Change of Character (reversal — first break against trend)
- **MSS** = Market Structure Shift (stronger CHoCH dengan displacement)
- **HH/HL** = Higher High / Higher Low (bullish)
- **LH/LL** = Lower High / Lower Low (bearish)

### Price Delivery
- **OB** = Order Block (last opposing candle before impulse)
- **FVG** = Fair Value Gap (3-candle imbalance)
- **IDM** = Inducement (minor swing = trap)

### ICT-Specific
- **IPDA** = Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm
- **AMD/Power of 3** = Accumulation → Manipulation → Distribution
- **Judas Swing** = False move early London
- **CRT** = Central Range Theory
- **SMT** = SMT Divergence (correlated pair)

## ✅ 12-Point Setup Validation Checklist

```
HTF Analysis:
□ 1. HTF bias confirmed? (Daily + 4H aligned)
□ 2. DOL identified? (BSL/SSL target marked)

Timing:
□ 3. Kill zone respected? (London/NY AM, bukan Asian)
□ 4. No high-impact news dalam 30 menit?

Price Action:
□ 5. Liquidity sweep occurred? (BSL/SSL taken)
□ 6. CHoCH confirmed on LTF? (15M typical)
□ 7. Displacement candle present? (large body)
□ 8. FVG atau OB on LTF identified?

Execution:
□ 9. Entry at FVG 50% / OB zone?
□ 10. SL beyond sweep wick? (5-15 pips buffer)
□ 11. R:R minimum 1:2? (ideally 1:3+)
□ 12. Position size calculated? (1-2% risk max)

Grade:
- 11-12 ✓ = A-grade (high confidence)
- 9-10  ✓ = B-grade (good, monitor tight)
- 7-8   ✓ = C-grade (skip, wait better)
- <7    ✓ = INVALID (do not trade)
```

## 🎯 Position Sizing Formula

```
Lot Size = (Account × Risk%) / (SL pips × Pip Value)
```

**Standard:**
- Risk 1-2% per trade max
- Standard lot XAUUSD = 100 oz
- Mini lot = 10 oz
- Micro lot = 1 oz
- Pip value per standard lot ≈ $1

**Example:**
- Account: $10,000
- Risk: 1% = $100
- SL: 50 pips
- Position: $100 / (50 × $1) = 0.2 lot (mini)

## 🛡️ Risk Management Rules

1. **Max risk 1-2% per trade**
2. **Max 3-5% total exposure** across all open trades
3. **Move SL ke BE** setelah TP1 hit
4. **Close 30-50% di TP1**, 30-40% di TP2, trail sisanya
5. **Daily loss limit 3%** — stop trading kalau hit
6. **Weekly loss limit 5%** — stop trading sampai next week
